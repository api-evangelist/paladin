The path from junior associate to experienced counsel often feels like a slow, meticulous climb. While billable work provides essential training, the highest-value opportunities, such as leading client conversations, arguing motions, or negotiating complex deals, often take years to reach. Now, we’re seeing professional development teams partnering on the new training tool: pro bono work.
